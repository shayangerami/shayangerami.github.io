**Multi-Branch Feature Fusion for Traffic Sign Recognition**

Traffic signs are defined by their edges, shapes, and colors. This project gives each cue its own frozen backbone and learns only how to fuse them.

#### Highlights

- Combined frozen edge, shape, and color backbones through feature-level fusion.
- Reached 86% accuracy on GTSRB with 1.2M trainable parameters and 16.8M frozen parameters.
