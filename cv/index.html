Projects

  • ReVisiT: Efficient Video Vision Transformers

    Manuscript under review

    • Developed training-free selective inference using luminance-based change detection, one-hop spatial expansion, and temporal activation caching to recompute selected tokens across video frames.
    • Evaluated ViTDet-B on ImageNet VID, achieving 82.27% mAP@0.5 versus 82.37% for dense inference while reducing estimated backbone operations by 69.3%.
    • Evaluated ViTPose-S/16 on Sub-JHMDB, achieving 95.77% PCK@0.2 versus 95.75% for dense inference with 64.6% fewer estimated backbone operations.
    • Integrated selective DINO ViT-S/16 inference into video object segmentation on DAVIS 2017, achieving 60.64% J&F versus 61.8% for dense inference with 33.8% fewer estimated backbone operations.
    • Adapted Eventful Transformers and STC-Cacher for pose and segmentation and MaskVD for segmentation. Conducted ablations on spatial expansion, periodic refresh, and attention update strategies to characterize accuracy–computation tradeoffs.
  • MGC: Missed-Guidance Correction for Diffusion and Flow Models

    Manuscript under review

    • Developed a training-free method for adapting diffusion and flow samplers to conditions arriving mid-generation. Estimated missed guidance from two to five historical probes and applied a single correction at the switch.
    • Investigated how historical guidance contributes target-specific information beyond the condition difference at the switch, and showed that successful semantic correction can occur without recovering the restart state.
    • Increased concept insertion success from 19.6% to 49.7% on Stable Diffusion 2.1 and from 41.4% to 71.0% on SD3.5 using two probes, averaged across switch times on restart-valid prompt pairs. Required 8% more model evaluations than direct switching and outperformed equal-compute rewinding by 26.1 percentage points on SD2.1.
    • Extended MGC to audio-visual speech enhancement with delayed lip-video conditioning, recovering 86.1% of the full audio-visual SI-SDR gain versus 49.4% for direct switching on the held-out speaker, and 72.9% versus 28.7% on unseen speakers and noise types.
  • SCENE: Sparse Video Reconstruction for Efficient Object Detection

    Manuscript under review

    • Built the system-level evaluation for a sparse event-driven in/near-sensor engine that reconstructs conventional video frames from selected changes for frame-based object detection.
    • Evaluated reconstruction on 30,402 ImageNet-VidVRD frames across 107 videos. Compared against full-frame processing, running-average background modeling, and ViBe.
    • Swept comparison thresholds, resolutions, and periodic full-frame refresh schedules, measuring image fidelity, modeled data transfer, and detector accuracy.
    • Integrated YOLOV++ inference and fine-tuning on reconstructed frames. Identified one configuration reaching 83% mAP@0.5 with 56% lower modeled transfer and another reaching 82% mAP@0.5 at 0.80 versus 2.76 MB/frame modeled transfer.

  • 2026
    ROAST: Reverse-training Offset Attack on Spatial Sampling Topologies

    ACM/IEEE DAC, Late Breaking Results

    • Developed a white-box attack on LBPNet that maximizes classification loss by updating learned spatial sampling offsets, then uses truncated Hamming distance on FP32 representations to construct a bit-flip schedule.
    • Evaluated independent and cumulative attacks on MNIST and SVHN, reducing accuracy by 69.52 and 64.42 percentage points while flipping only 4.39% and 4.52% of offset bits, respectively. Achieved greater accuracy degradation per flipped bit than BFA.
